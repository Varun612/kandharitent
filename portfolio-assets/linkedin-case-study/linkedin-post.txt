Most local event businesses run on scattered WhatsApp chats, paper diaries, and manual price calculations. 

I engineered a full-stack digital platform for Kandhari Tent & Caterers and Dwarka Shri Lawn (Lucknow, UP) that transformed their entire operation from a manual workflow into an automated, end-to-end digital business.

Here is the engineering breakdown of what I built and deployed to production:

🌐 Live Application: https://kandharitent.vercel.app/
📄 Swipe through the PDF carousel below for the system architecture, database schema, and live admin screens!

---

💡 1. The Core Engineering Challenges:
• High-friction rental quoting: Rental equipment orders (luxury tents, banquet seating, German hangers, decorative lighting) require dynamic itemized configuration, security deposits, and real-time 18% GST tax computation.
• Dual-domain consolidation: Merging an equipment rental store, luxury catering custom menu builder, and a physical event venue (Dwarka Shri Lawn) with 4K video reel walkthroughs into a sub-second, mobile-first experience.
• Non-technical admin usability: The back-office operators needed an intuitive dashboard to manage staff daily attendance, track vendor payouts, review quotes, update inventory, and approve booking dates without needing any technical training.

---

⚙️ 2. Architectural Decisions & Tech Stack:
• Frontend: Built with React 18, Vite 5, and Tailwind CSS. Employs modular component decomposition, custom hooks for persistent cart state, responsive video containers with fallback poster art, and fluid micro-interactions using Lucide icons.
• Backend API: Node.js & Express 4 structured around REST principles. Features a custom dual-mount routing engine (mountDualRoute) that maps legacy endpoints seamlessly alongside structured /api/v1 routes with zero breaking changes.
• Authentication & Security: JWT-based stateless authentication with bcrypt password hashing (10 salt rounds), secure role-based route guards, and payload validation.
• Dual-Storage Resilient Data Tier: Designed an automatic fallback engine that syncs to MongoDB Atlas in cloud production while seamlessly defaulting to a synchronous, atomic local JSON datastore during offline or zero-network operations.

---

📊 3. Key Business Modules Delivered:
1. Interactive Rental E-Commerce Engine: Real-time quoting cart with automated subtotal, 18% GST calculation, and instant WhatsApp payload dispatch.
2. Dwarka Shri Lawn Showcase: Embedded reel player highlighting the 50,000 sq ft venue, stage sets, and dining zones with zero layout shift.
3. Operations Hub: 11-section secured back-office managing:
   - Real-time KPI analytics & revenue overview
   - Staff daily attendance & payroll ledger
   - Outside vendor expense tracker
   - Customer CRM & multi-stage booking pipeline
   - Live inventory tracking & equipment availability
   - Automated invoice and billing generator

---

Building this reinforced an essential truth: Great software engineering isn't just about using modern libraries—it's about understanding real-world operational bottlenecks and building resilient, human-centered systems that solve them reliably.
